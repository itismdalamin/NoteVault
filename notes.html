About Us Privacy Policy

About Us

NoteVault is a small, focused tool for handing a note to someone else without setting up an account, a login, or a shared drive. Write it, get a three-word phrase, and pass that phrase along however you already talk to that person.

Why three words

A phrase built from three shuffled words is easy to read aloud, easy to type on a phone, and — with our word sets combined — spread across billions of combinations, so notes don't collide.

What we're not

We're not a permanent archive. Every note carries an expiry you choose, and once it lapses the content is no longer reachable through the app.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what happens to the text you write in NoteVault in plain terms.

What we store

The content of your note, the three-word phrase tied to it, and the expiry time you set are stored in our database so the note can sync in real time and be reopened with the phrase.

Who can access a note

Anyone who has the exact three-word phrase can view and edit that note. Treat the phrase like a password — anyone you share it with can read and change the content until it expires.

Expiry & deletion

Once a note's expiry time passes, it's no longer accessible through the app. Deleting a note or letting it expire removes it from active use.

What we don't do

We don't require an account, we don't ask for your name or email to use the note-taking features, and we don't sell note content.

Open source

NoteVault is an open-source project — the full source code is publicly available for anyone to read, audit, or self-host. You can review exactly how notes are stored and handled on GitHub.
